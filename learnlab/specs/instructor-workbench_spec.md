# Specification: Instructor Workbench

## Overview
Instructors manage their content and view aggregated insights about learner progress in their courses.

## Acceptance Criteria
- AC-WORKBENCH-01: Instructor can author courses with modules, lessons, and quiz questions.
- AC-WORKBENCH-02: Instructor can view learner progress aggregated by course.
- AC-WORKBENCH-03: Progress aggregation must show count of learners by progress band (0-25%, 25-50%, 50-75%, 75-100%).
- AC-WORKBENCH-04: Instructor role is separated at the controller layer and protected from Learner/Admin roles.
