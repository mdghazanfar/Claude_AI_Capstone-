# Specification: Enrollment & Learning

## Overview
Learners enroll in published courses and progress through modules and lessons sequentially.

## Acceptance Criteria
- AC-ENROLL-01: Learner can register and enroll in any PUBLISHED course.
- AC-ENROLL-02: Learner cannot enroll twice in the same course.
- AC-ENROLL-03: Learner can only enroll in courses that are in PUBLISHED state.
- AC-ENROLL-04: Learner can move through modules and lessons sequentially.
- AC-ENROLL-05: Learner can mark a lesson as complete.
- AC-ENROLL-06: Lesson completions and enrollment records are stored as append-only events.
